Peru Itinerary

Three weeks of Inca ruins, mountain trails, island adventures, sand dunes and the Amazon jungle.

  1. Aug 30-31 BART → SFO → Lima → Cusco (about 27 hours of travel)
  2. Sep 1 Look around Cusco
  3. Sep 2 ATV Trip to the Sacred Valley and Maras Salt Mines near Cusco
  4. Sep 3 Start hiking the Inca Trail
  5. Sep 4 Day 2 on the trail, cross Dead Womans Pass
  6. Sep 5 Day 3 on the the Inca Trail
  7. Sep 6 Arrive at Machu Picchu spend night in Aguas Calientes
  8. Sep 7 Climb Huayna Picchu
  9. Sep 8 Travel by Bus to Puno and Lake Titicaca
  10. Sep 9 Travel by boat on Lake Titicaca to visit the Uros Floating Islands and Amantaní Island. Stay with a family on Amantaní Island.
  11. Sep 10 Take the boat back to Puno.
  12. Sep 11 Fly to Lima.
  13. Sep 12 Sandboarding at Chilca Dunes.
  14. Sep 13 Making Chocolate class in Lima.
  15. Sep 14 Fly to Iquitos and boat to Tahuayo Lodge on the Amazon.
  16. Sep 15 - 18 Activities at Tahuayo Lodge.
  17. Sep 19 - 21 Activities at the Amazon Research Center.
  18. Sep 22 Return to Iquitos and fly home.
