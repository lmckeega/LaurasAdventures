Hotel Marquesas

This is where we are staying for our first 3 nights in Cusco. It is a beautiful and historic hotel. The people are very nice. They have a free breakfast buffet every morning.

When we first arrived, they brought us a cup of coca tea and gave us some recommendations for adjusting to the altitude. They were, have a cup of tea, sleep for a little while and take the first day easy. We did just that!
